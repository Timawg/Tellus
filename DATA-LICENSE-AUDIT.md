# Tellus commercial data audit

Reviewed November 6, 2026 against Tellus commit `75e6ba1`. The audit covers sources called by the iOS app, data bundled in it, and the H3 dependency used to display GPSJAM. It distinguishes the rights to use the data from the terms for calling the hosted service. The findings assume a public commercial release, including an app supported by ads or subscriptions.

## License or permission required

## Release priorities

1. Address OpenSky, the current Open-Meteo endpoints, WHO summaries, TeleGeography, adsbdb routes, and the Smithsonian endpoint. Both live flights and natural hazards are enabled by default, and several country-guide sources load automatically. Remove unauthorized calls from a commercial build even when their output would be hidden.
2. Resolve GPSJAM, visa CSV provenance, IODA, aircraft registration and photo rights, and production access to OpenInfraMap tiles. Turning a layer off by default still leaves it available in the release.
3. Put provider credits and license links in the app where the data is shown, and keep copies of provider permissions. Apple’s App Review guideline 5.2.2 covers third-party services and may require proof of authorization. [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#intellectual-property)
